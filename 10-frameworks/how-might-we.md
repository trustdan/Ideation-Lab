# How Might We
## When to use
Turning problems, complaints, or research insights into generative prompts; also the repair
tool when a group is stuck arguing about solutions.
## Steps
1. State the problem/insight. 2. Each person writes 3–5 "How might we…" reframes (vary
scope: one broader, one narrower, one inverted). 3. Cluster on the wall; dot-vote the 2–3
most generative. 4. Winning HMWs seed the next divergent block.
## Output
2–3 selected HMW prompts, captured; often feeds [[crazy-8s]].
## Facilitation notes
Reject HMWs that smuggle in a solution ("HMW build an app that…"). Breadth test: can you
imagine 10 different answers?

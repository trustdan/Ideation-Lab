# Design sprint: day by day
## When to use
Orienting a team new to Design Sprint methodology, before adapting any agenda to this pack's
real constraints.
## Steps
1. **Monday — Map**: long-term goal, sprint questions, expert interviews, pick a target.
2. **Tuesday — Sketch**: HMWs, Crazy 8s, solo storyboards.
3. **Wednesday — Decide**: silent critique, dot vote, decider's supervote, storyboard the
   winner.
4. **Thursday — Prototype**: fake it till you make it — a façade, not a working build.
5. **Friday — Test**: five customer interviews against the prototype, live synthesis.
## Output
Shared vocabulary and a mental model of the whole arc before diving into any single day's
blocks.
## Facilitation notes
This is Knapp's original five-day structure. `[[design-sprint-2-agenda]]` compresses it to
four days — say so explicitly, or a team expects a fifth day that isn't on the calendar. This

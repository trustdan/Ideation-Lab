# Time recovery playbook
## When to use
Any block is running over and the day's remaining blocks won't fit — before silently cutting
the wrong thing.
## Steps
1. Cut informal share-outs and gallery walks first — lowest unique output per minute.
2. Compress a voting round to one silent pass, no re-vote.
3. Merge two adjacent convergence blocks into one.
4. Shorten a premortem to the top 2 causes only, skip the round-robin.
5. Protect the decide gate and the test day above everything else — they never get cut.
## Output
A protected core (decide + test) and a named list of what got cut, for the retro.
## Facilitation notes
Announce the cut out loud. A silent trim erodes trust more than an honest "we're cutting X to
protect Y." No affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart is implied.

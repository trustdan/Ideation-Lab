# Time recovery playbook
## When to use
Any block is running over and the day's remaining blocks won't fit — before silently cutting
the wrong thing.
## Steps
1. **~10 min behind:** cut informal share-outs and gallery walks first — lowest unique output
   per minute; convert spoken sharing to silent writing or a single dot-vote pass.
2. **~20 min behind:** pause the room explicitly, name the shortfall, and drop an optional
   middle exercise (e.g. skip the gallery walk, go straight to synthesis/decision).
3. **~40 min behind:** call a hard timeout, tell the group the real time left, and hand the
   call to the decider — extend, schedule a follow-up, or compress the target output. Their
   call, not a unilateral cut.
4. Compress a voting round to one silent pass, no re-vote. Merge two adjacent convergence
   blocks into one. Shorten a premortem to the top 2 causes only, skip the round-robin.
5. Protect the decide gate and the test day above everything else — they never get cut.
## Output
A protected core (decide + test) and a named list of what got cut, for the retro.
## Facilitation notes
Announce the cut out loud: "we have 20 minutes left and 2 steps remaining — I propose we skip
X and move straight to Y. Any objection?" A silent trim erodes trust more than an honest
"we're cutting X to protect Y." Build 10–15% buffer into every agenda up front, and mark
optional modules as cuttable in advance so this isn't decided live. No affiliation with GV or
AJ&Smart is implied.

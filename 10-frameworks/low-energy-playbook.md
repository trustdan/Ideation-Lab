# Low-energy room playbook
## When to use
Slouching, yawning, minimal detail on sticky notes, long silences after open questions —
usually a post-lunch dip, too much passive listening, or unclear purpose on the current
activity. Ask: is this physical fatigue or conceptual confusion, and have people been seated
60+ minutes straight?
## Steps
1. Get people out of their chairs immediately. 2. Switch from full-group discussion to pair
work. 3. Take a strict 5-minute movement break. Script: "Our energy's dipping, which is normal
after a dense morning — let's stand up and do a 2-minute pair round-robin." 4. If that's not
enough, run a short physical energizer or cut a non-essential presentation and move straight
to the interactive block.
## Output
Re-engaged room; no file artifact.
## Facilitation notes
Don't push through a packed agenda while everyone's exhausted, and don't force fun that
embarrasses people. Prevent upstream: cap passive presentation blocks at 15–20 minutes and
build a movement break into the agenda every 60–90 minutes.

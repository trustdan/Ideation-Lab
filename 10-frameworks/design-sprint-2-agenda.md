# Design sprint 2.0 agenda
## When to use
Planning a Design Sprint 2.0 pack — the four-day compression of
`[[design-sprint-day-by-day|Knapp's original five days]]`.
## Steps
1. **Day 1 — Map & Sketch** (~7h): long-term goal, sprint questions, expert interviews, HMWs,
   target pick, Crazy 8s, solo storyboards.
2. **Day 2 — Decide** (~6h): silent critique, `[[dot-voting]]`, straw poll, decider's
   supervote, storyboard the winner.
3. **Day 3 — Prototype** (~6h): façade build, prep the `[[five-act-interview]]` script.
4. **Day 4 — Test** (~5h): five interviews, live synthesis of what was learned.
## Output
A four-day block-by-block schedule the facilitator adapts to the room's real constraints —
this is a bias to seed from, never a script to run verbatim.
## Facilitation notes
The whole point of 2.0 vs. classic is merging Monday and Tuesday into one day — don't let Day
1 balloon back to two days out of caution. No affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart is implied.

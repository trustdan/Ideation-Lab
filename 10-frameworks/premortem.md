# Premortem
## When to use
A favorite has emerged and momentum is drowning doubt — right before commitment.
## Steps
1. "It's 12 months later; this failed completely." 2. Everyone writes causes of death
silently, 5 min. 3. Round-robin, cluster, rank by probability × severity. 4. Top 3 get a
cheap kill test or mitigation with an owner.
## Output
Ranked failure causes + kill tests → feed the provocateur's challenge note and
`50-actions.md`.
## Facilitation notes
Past tense is mandatory ("it failed", not "it might"). The quietest skeptic goes first.

# 90-min ideation agenda
## When to use
Taking a group from an existing problem statement/HMW through rapid divergent sketching to a
prioritized shortlist ready for prototyping. Requires the HMW to already exist —
`[[agenda-60min-problem-framing]]` frames the problem, this one doesn't.
## Steps
1. **0–10m Framing & check-in.** 2. **10–25m Lightning inspiration** — 2-min example
share-outs (cut to 5m if pre-reads were sent). 3. **25–40m `[[crazy-8s]]`.** 4. **40–60m
Solution sketching** — best idea from the 8, fleshed into one detailed 3-panel sketch,
silent. 5. **60–75m Silent gallery walk + heat-map dot vote.** 6. **75–85m Speed critique +
decider supervote.** 7. **85–90m Assign owners** for the winning concept(s).
## Output
6–10 detailed solution sketches and a decider-approved shortlist ready for prototyping.
## Facilitation notes
Do not compress the silent solution-sketching block below 15 minutes — rushed sketches turn
into vague sticky notes instead of concrete concepts. If people freeze up on drawing,
reassure them stick figures and text boxes are valid; clarity beats artistic skill. No
affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart is implied.

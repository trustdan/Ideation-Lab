# Five-act interview
## When to use
Friday's user test — validating the prototype against the sprint questions with real target
users, one at a time.
## Steps
1. **Friendly welcome** — put them at ease, no leading framing.
2. **Context questions** — their world and habits, before showing anything.
3. **Introduce the prototype** — frame it as unfinished: "tell me what you see."
4. **Detailed tasks** — a scenario, think-aloud throughout.
5. **Quick debrief** — rapid-fire reactions, thank them.
## Output
One interview's worth of think-aloud notes → `/capture`; across five interviews, feeds
directly into the session's `test-scenario` verdicts.
## Facilitation notes
One interviewer, one note-taker minimum; the rest of the team watches live where possible —
reactions in the room are as valuable as the transcript. No affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart
is implied.

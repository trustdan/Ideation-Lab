# Crazy 8s
## When to use
Early divergence; you need volume and variety fast, before anyone gets precious.
## Steps
1. Each person folds paper into 8 panels. 2. 8 sketches/one-liners in 8 minutes — one idea
per panel, quantity over quality. 3. 3-min silent gallery walk. 4. Each person reads their
strongest 2 aloud (30s each) → straight into `/capture`.
## Output
8 × headcount raw idea fragments in `20-capture/`.
## Facilitation notes
Enforce the timer loudly; discomfort is the mechanism. Ban "I'm not creative" disclaimers.

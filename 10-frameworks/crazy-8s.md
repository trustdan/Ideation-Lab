# Crazy 8s
## When to use
Early divergence; you need volume and variety fast, before anyone gets precious. Not before
the group agrees on the problem, and not for decisions needing sustained technical depth.
## Steps
1. Each person folds paper into 8 panels (skip for digital: pre-drawn 2×4 grid). 2. 8
sketches/one-liners in 8 minutes — one idea per panel, quantity over quality; announce time
at the 4-, 2-, and 1-minute marks. 3. 3-min silent gallery walk. 4. Each person reads their
strongest 2 aloud (30s each) → straight into `/capture`.
## Output
8 × headcount raw idea fragments in `20-capture/`.
## Facilitation notes
Enforce the timer loudly; discomfort is the mechanism. Ban "I'm not creative" disclaimers —
say up front that stick figures and words count and quality is irrelevant. If someone stalls
after 3–4 panels, don't let them invent a new idea from scratch: "take your last idea and
change one element, or imagine its opposite." Remote: mouse-drawing is slower — add 2–4
minutes, or let people drag/drop icons instead of drawing.

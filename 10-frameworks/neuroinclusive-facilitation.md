# Neuroinclusive facilitation
## When to use
Every session, by default — universal-design adaptations for ADHD, autism, dyslexia, anxiety,
and processing differences improve the experience for everyone, not just the participants
they're named for.
## Steps
1. Send the agenda, goals, and exercise prompts 24–48h ahead; no pop-quiz prompts. 2. State
norms early: cameras optional during writing, standing/pacing/fidgeting welcome. 3. Give
instructions multi-modal (spoken + written + visual) and one operational step at a time,
never stacked — "write three ideas, rank them, then discuss" is three instructions, not one.
4. Default to silent individual writing before verbal sharing. 5. Use visible timers, and give
brief extensions to anyone actively still writing. 6. Watch the physical environment
(lighting, scent, ambient noise) and normalize movement without requiring permission.
## Output
No separate artifact — this is a standing modifier on every other framework in this library.
## Facilitation notes
Never cold-call. Fidgeting, looking away, or pacing is often how someone maintains focus —
don't police body language or read it as disengagement. Grounded in CAST's Universal Design
for Learning principles.

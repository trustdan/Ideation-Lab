# 60-min problem-framing agenda
## When to use
Moving a cross-functional team from a vague challenge to one aligned, actionable problem
statement in an hour. Requires a decider in the room and, ideally, background context shared
24h ahead.
## Steps
1. **0–5m Framing & check-in.** 2. **5–15m Silent problem dump** — sticky notes on pain
points, no talking. 3. **15–25m Affinity clustering** — group and label themes in silence. 4.
**25–40m `[[how-might-we|HMW]]` generation** from the clusters. 5. **40–50m Dot vote**, then
the decider picks the primary HMW. 6. **50–60m** Refine the chosen HMW, confirm owner and
next step.
## Output
A categorized pain-point board, a set of HMWs, and one decider-approved problem statement.
## Facilitation notes
Check-in can trim to 2 minutes if time is tight; silent writing and the final decider pick
must not be cut — skipping silent writing biases toward the loudest voice, skipping the
decider pick leaves the choice unresolved. If the room splits between two problems near the
end, have the decider choose immediately and park the runner-up as "phase 2 scope." No
affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart is implied.

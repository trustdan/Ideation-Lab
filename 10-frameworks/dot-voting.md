# Dot voting
## When to use
Cheap convergence on a visible pool (ideas, themes, HMWs) when you need a shortlist, not a
final decision.
## Steps
1. Post all candidates. 2. Criteria on the wall (one sentence — vote on X). 3. Each person
gets N dots (N ≈ candidates/3), placed silently; ≤2 dots on one item. 4. Read the top
cluster aloud; brief "anything mis-ranked?" round.
## Output
A shortlist with visible weight; record counts in `/capture`.
## Facilitation notes
Silent placement, leaders vote last or not at all. It's a signal, not a verdict — decisions
still go through the synthesis and `50-actions.md`.

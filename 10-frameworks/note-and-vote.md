# Note and vote
## When to use
Converging on a shortlist or a single direction without letting the loudest voice anchor the
room.
## Steps
1. Silent writing — each person notes their pick and a one-line reason.
2. Silent posting — no discussion yet.
3. Silent dot vote (`[[dot-voting]]`, scoped to the written notes, not raw ideas).
4. Open discussion only after voting, starting with the least-voted note.
## Output
A ranked shortlist where each pick's stated reason survives alongside its tally, not just the
count.
## Facilitation notes
The silence is the mechanism — this exists because plain `[[dot-voting]]` loses the "why,"
and an ungrounded shortlist is a real failure mode this vault has already seen. No
affiliation with GV or AJ&Smart is implied.

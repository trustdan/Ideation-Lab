# Voting-tie playbook
## When to use
A prioritization round (e.g. `[[dot-voting]]`) produces two or more options tied for the top
spot and the group is stuck.
## Steps
1. If a decider is in the room, hand them the tie-break supervote directly. 2. No decider: one
60-second pitch per tied option, then everyone gets exactly one tie-breaker vote. 3. Check
whether the options are actually mutually exclusive, or one is just step 1 of the other —
combine instead of choosing. Script: "We have a tie between A and B — per our group roles,
[decider] makes the final call."
## Output
A single winning option, no extended debate.
## Facilitation notes
Don't let this turn into an untimeboxed push for 100% consensus — the whole point of naming a
decider up front is to have this exact moment resolved in under two minutes.

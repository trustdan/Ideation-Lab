# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW decide who leads based on who's actually best positioned to help the person in front of
  us, not on which org's charter mentions it first?
- HMW coordinate in a way that doesn't cost either org funding it depends on?
- HMW close the gap where someone needing two services from two orgs currently gets neither?
- HMW give the smaller or newer org in the room a real say in how the mandate gets divided?
- HMW make a referral between our organizations something that actually happens, not just
  something that's technically possible?
- HMW tell a genuine mission overlap apart from two orgs each wanting credit for the same work?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "Think of a real person who needed help from two organizations in this room. What actually
  happened to them?"
- "If a funder announced tomorrow that only coordinated applications would be considered, what
  would we wish we'd already agreed to?"
- "What's a service area where our organizations are quietly competing for the same clients
  right now?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one referral you made to another organization in this room that you never found out the
  outcome of."

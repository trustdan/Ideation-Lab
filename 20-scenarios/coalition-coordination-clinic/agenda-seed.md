# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~25m — reframe "whose mandate is this" into HMW stems scoped to the
   person needing the service, not to either org's charter language.
2. [[note-and-vote]] — ~30m — for each overlapping mandate area on the list, silent-write a
   position and reason before open discussion, so the larger or better-funded org doesn't
   anchor the room first.
3. [[gradients-of-agreement]] — ~40m — run the decide gate per mandate area on the 8-point
   scale; a 5–6 from a smaller org is a real signal to capture, not overridden by headcount.
4. [[premortem]] — ~30m — pressure-test the leading coordination bets against the funding
   incentive misalignment named at the open — what happens when grant season makes cooperation
   costly again.
5. [[commitment-round]] — ~30m — per bet: named lead org, first concrete action, and date.
   Explicitly log anything parked as out-of-scope (turf, funding formulas) rather than letting
   it drop silently.

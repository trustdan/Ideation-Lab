# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW win on something other than price against a cheaper competitor?
- HMW find the one segment where our current motion is already compounding, and double down
  before diluting focus elsewhere?
- HMW turn our weakest disqualifier into a wedge for a different buyer profile?
- HMW shorten the sales cycle in our stalled segment without discounting to do it?
- HMW build a positioning claim reps actually believe, not just one marketing signed off on?
- HMW learn what's really driving the stalled segment before assuming it's the product?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If our newest competitor doubled their sales team next quarter, what would we wish we'd
  already fixed about our own pitch?"
- "What are we still pitching only because 'that's our positioning' — not because it's still
  winning deals?"
- "If a competitor with a worse product but a sharper story took our next flagship renewal,
  what would they have gotten right that we didn't?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one deal we lost in the last two quarters where the loss reason in the CRM wasn't the
  real reason."

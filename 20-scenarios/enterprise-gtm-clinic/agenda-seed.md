# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~30m — reframe "how do we hit number" into concrete HMW stems (see
   prompt-pack.md) before anyone is allowed to propose a tactic.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — rapid-fire positioning/segment sketches against the strongest 2–3 HMW
   stems from the previous block.
3. [[dot-voting]] — ~15m — converge the sketch pile to the 2–3 strongest GTM bets, with reps
   and leadership voting separately before combining tallies so neither group's view is
   swamped by headcount.
4. [[premortem]] — ~30m — pressure-test the leading bet before it hardens into a plan; aim the
   room's risk instincts at the *chosen* bet, not at killing divergence earlier in the day.
5. [[commitment-round]] — ~25m — named owner, first concrete pipeline/enablement action, and a
   date, per committed bet.

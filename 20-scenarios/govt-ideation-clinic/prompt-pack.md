# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW pilot this within our actual procurement authority, without waiting on a new contract
  vehicle?
- HMW test an idea small enough that a public misstep would be a footnote, not a headline?
- HMW find the career staff member who'd sponsor this past the next administration or
  leadership change?
- HMW learn what residents/constituents actually experience today, not what the process
  document says they experience?
- HMW borrow an approach another agency or jurisdiction already piloted, instead of starting
  from a blank page?
- HMW give this initiative a kill test specific enough that "it's still going" isn't mistaken
  for "it's working"?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If a reporter wrote about this initiative failing, what's the version of that story we could
  live with?"
- "What are we still doing only because 'that's the process' — not because anyone's confirmed
  it's actually required?"
- "If leadership changed next year, would this initiative survive, or does it only exist
  because of who's in the room today?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one idea from a past working group that everyone agreed was good and nothing ever
  happened to."

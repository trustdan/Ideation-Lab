# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~25m — reframe the challenge into HMW stems (see prompt-pack.md) with
   real procurement/legal boundaries pre-cleared, so the room doesn't self-censor on
   unconfirmed constraints.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — rapid-fire initiative sketches against the strongest 2–3 HMW stems.
3. [[dot-voting]] — ~15m — converge the sketch pile to the top 2–3 candidate initiatives.
4. [[premortem]] — ~25m — pressure-test the leading initiatives, specifically for public
   accountability risk and for who owns each one past the next leadership transition.
5. [[commitment-round]] — ~25m — named owner, first concrete action, a dated check-in, and an
   explicit kill test per committed initiative.

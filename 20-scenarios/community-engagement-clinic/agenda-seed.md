# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~30m — reframe "why isn't the community using this" into HMW stems (see
   prompt-pack.md) that assume a structural barrier exists until disproven, not an awareness
   gap.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — sketch concrete engagement/service changes against the strongest 2–3
   HMW stems.
3. [[note-and-vote]] — ~20m — converge via silent write-then-vote so community voices and
   frontline staff aren't drowned out by program leadership's framing.
4. [[premortem]] — ~25m — pressure-test the leading change against the room's account of the
   real structural barrier, not the version staff would prefer it to be.
5. [[commitment-round]] — ~20m — named pilot, first concrete action, and a date to check back
   with the community on whether it actually helped.

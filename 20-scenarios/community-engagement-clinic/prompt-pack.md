# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW remove a structural barrier (transportation, hours, language, documentation) instead of
  asking people to work around it?
- HMW rebuild trust with a community that's had a bad experience with this institution before,
  not just with this program?
- HMW make eligibility and enrollment obvious to someone who's never dealt with a program like
  this before?
- HMW learn what people are actually doing instead of using this service, and why that's
  working better for them?
- HMW give community members a real say in what changes, not just a survey after the fact?
- HMW tell the difference between "they don't know about it" and "they know about it and
  decided against it for a reason"?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If you needed this service yourself, with no institutional knowledge of how it works, where
  would you give up?"
- "What's one thing this program asks of people that we'd never ask of someone with more
  resources or more trust in the system?"
- "If the community redesigned this program with no input from us, what would they change
  first?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one piece of feedback from the community that the program has heard more than once and
  hasn't acted on."

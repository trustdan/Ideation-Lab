# References
<!-- Optional pre-reads and background links. -->

Bring, don't fabricate — pull these from the specific program before the session:
- Uptake/enrollment data, and any breakdown by demographic or geography that exists.
- Direct community feedback — complaints, surveys, advisory-council notes, exit interviews from
  people who disengaged.
- Any prior engagement or outreach attempts and why they didn't move the needle.
- A clear-eyed account of the program's actual eligibility/enrollment requirements as
  experienced by an applicant, not as described in program materials.

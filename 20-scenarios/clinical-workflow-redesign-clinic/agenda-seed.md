# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~25m — reframe "why doesn't anyone follow the process" into HMW stems
   (see prompt-pack.md) that assume the workaround is rational until proven otherwise.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — sketch concrete workflow changes against the strongest 2–3 HMW stems.
3. [[note-and-vote]] — ~20m — converge via silent write-then-vote, not open discussion, so
   role hierarchy doesn't decide the shortlist before the room does.
4. [[premortem]] — ~25m — pressure-test the leading change against the unit's actual hard
   safety constraints (pre-cleared before the session, not litigated live).
5. [[commitment-round]] — ~20m — named pilot unit or shift, first concrete action, and a date
   to review whether the pilot is working.

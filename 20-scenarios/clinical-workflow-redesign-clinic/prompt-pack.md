# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW make the compliant way faster than the workaround, instead of asking people to be more
  disciplined?
- HMW find out what the workaround protects against that the official process doesn't?
- HMW redesign the handoff that most often loses information between shifts?
- HMW give the role closest to the workaround a real say in what replaces it?
- HMW tell a true hard safety constraint apart from a soft objection dressed up as one?
- HMW pilot a change on one shift without asking every shift to adopt it untested?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If a new hire followed the official process exactly as written, on their first shift, what
  would go wrong that everyone experienced already knows to avoid?"
- "What's a workaround you'd be embarrassed to explain to a surveyor — and what does that tell
  us about the official process, not about the person doing it?"
- "If the next incident report names this workflow, what will it say we already knew?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one step in your daily routine that exists only because of something that happened
  once, years ago."

# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW make the next step obvious to a patient who just left a referral appointment confused?
- HMW close the pathway that loses the most patients, even if it's not the one we talk about
  most?
- HMW remove a barrier (transportation, language, cost) instead of asking the patient to work
  around it?
- HMW make the handoff between departments someone's job, not everyone's assumption?
- HMW find out where in the pipeline we're actually losing people when we don't have clean data
  to point to?
- HMW turn a missed appointment into information instead of just a statistic?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If you were a patient with no car, no English fluency, and a job you couldn't miss, where in
  this pathway would you fall out?"
- "What's the step in this pathway that exists for our convenience, not the patient's?"
- "If a competing system offered same-week scheduling for this same referral, would we lose
  this patient — and do we already know that?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one patient story — yours or a colleague's — where the system, not the patient, caused
  the missed appointment."

# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~30m — reframe "what technology should we adopt" into HMW stems (see
   prompt-pack.md) anchored to a real capability gap, not to a specific technology someone
   already wants.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — rapid-fire pilot/pathway sketches against the strongest 2–3 HMW stems.
3. [[dot-voting]] — ~15m — converge the sketch pile to the 2–3 strongest candidate pilots.
4. [[bet-brief]] — ~35m — write up each shortlisted pilot: the capability gap, the pathway
   (OTA, SBIR/STTR, existing vehicle), the resourcing ask, and the minimum viable scope.
5. [[premortem]] — ~30m — pressure-test the leading bet specifically against requirements creep
   and program-of-record defensiveness, not just technical risk.
6. [[commitment-round]] — ~25m — named owner, first concrete action, and a decision date for
   whether the pilot earns further investment.

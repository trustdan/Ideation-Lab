# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW pilot a real capability gap with a non-traditional vendor without waiting for a full
  program of record?
- HMW keep a pilot's scope small enough to actually finish before requirements creep kills it?
- HMW get an operator's honest reaction to a prototype before it's polished enough to look
  finished?
- HMW use an OTA or SBIR/STTR pathway to test something a formal RFP process would take two
  years to even solicit?
- HMW tell a genuine technical risk apart from a program-of-record's reflexive defense of the
  status quo?
- HMW learn from the last pilot that stalled out, instead of repeating whatever killed it?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If a peer service or ally nation fielded this capability eighteen months from now, what
  would we wish we'd already piloted?"
- "What's the smallest version of this idea that would still tell us whether it works — and why
  aren't we scoping to that?"
- "If this pilot reported to a program office with no stake in the current program of record,
  would it still get killed at the same point it usually does?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one prototype or pilot from the last two years that never got a real decision — it just
  quietly stopped."

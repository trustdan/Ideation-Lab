# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW reduce our dependence on our top prime without damaging that relationship?
- HMW position for the shift from LPTA toward best-value / OTA-style awards?
- HMW win our first prime contract in an adjacent mission area within 18 months?
- HMW turn our small-business / socioeconomic status from a ceiling into a wedge?
- HMW retain cleared talent when larger primes can out-bid us on comp?
- HMW build a past-performance story for a customer we've never worked for?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If our top two primes both cut orders 30% next fiscal year, what would we already wish
  we'd started six months ago?"
- "What are we still doing only because 'that's how we've always won work' — not because it
  still works?"
- "If a competitor with no past performance in our space won our flagship recompete, what
  would they have had to be right about?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one contract we lost in the last two years where the debrief told us something we
  never actually acted on."

# Capture templates
<!-- Facilitator-facing formatting guide for `/capture` during specific sprint moments — not
a new note type. Captures stay `type: capture`; this just keeps entries consistent enough for
`/distill` to work with. -->

## HMW
`HMW <stem>? — <one-line source: expert-interview insight, warm-up answer, etc.>`

## Lightning demo
`Lightning demo: <what was shown> — <the one borrowable pattern, in one line> — <source, e.g.
"competitor X" or "unrelated industry Y">`

## Dot-vote outcome
`Dot vote — <round name, e.g. "storyboard critique">: <item> = <dot count>`
One line per item; tally, don't editorialize — the synthesis is where weight gets interpreted.

## Test-interview note
`Interview <#>/5, Act <1-5>: <think-aloud quote or observed behavior, verbatim where
possible> — <flag: confirms/contradicts sprint question ts-0X, if clear in the moment>`

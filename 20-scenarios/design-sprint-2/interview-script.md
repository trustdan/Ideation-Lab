# Interview script — design sprint 2.0

## Before each interview
- Confirm which prototype path this interviewee will walk. If the winning storyboard (Day 2)
  branches into more than one flow, assign or rotate flows across the five interviewees so no
  single path goes untested.
- Have the sprint questions in front of you. Every interview should produce evidence toward at
  least one `test-scenario` verdict — `pending` moving to `validated` / `refuted` /
  `inconclusive` per `90-meta/taxonomy.md`, which requires a linked `evidence:` entry for any
  non-`pending` call.
- One interviewer, one note-taker minimum, per the framework's own facilitation note; the rest
  of the team watches live where the room allows — their reactions are as much signal as the
  transcript.
- Capture using `capture-templates.md`'s Test-interview line format so `/distill` and the Day 4
  live synthesis can work from consistent notes:
  `Interview <#>/5, Act <1-5>: <quote/behavior> — <flag: confirms/contradicts ts-0X>`.

## Act 1 — Friendly welcome
Per the framework: put them at ease, no leading framing.
Script line: *"Thanks for making time today — there's no right or wrong answer here. We're
testing a design, not you, so the more blunt you can be, the more useful this is for us."*

## Act 2 — Context questions
Per the framework: their world and habits, before showing anything. For this pack, lead with
the prompt-pack's own warm-up question — *"Describe the last time you personally worked
around this [product/process] instead of using it as intended — what did you actually do?"* —
plus whatever the screener surfaced about their current workaround. This baseline is what Act
4's think-aloud gets compared against; don't skip it under time pressure.

## Act 3 — Introduce the prototype
Per the framework: frame it as unfinished, ask what they see before explaining anything.
Script line: *"This is very early — think of it as a rough sketch, not a finished product.
Before I say anything else: what do you think this is?"* If one of the sprint questions is
about unaided comprehension of a core mechanic, this act — not Act 4 — is where that evidence
comes from; resist the urge to explain.

## Act 4 — Detailed tasks
Per the framework: a scenario, think-aloud throughout. Map one task per sprint question so
each maps cleanly to a `test-scenario` note. Give the scenario, not the mechanic — e.g. *"Say
you wanted to [job-to-be-done]. Show me what you'd do, talking through your thinking as you
go."* Let silence sit; don't rescue them from confusion — confusion is data.

## Act 5 — Quick debrief
Per the framework: rapid-fire reactions, thank them. Close with one open question that catches
whatever the scripted tasks didn't: *"If you could change one thing about what you just saw,
what would it be?"* — this often surfaces the sharpest unscripted evidence of the interview.

## After all five interviews
Roll the five interviews' captures up against each sprint question during the Day 4 live
synthesis block (agenda-seed.md, Day 4 step 2): tally confirms/contradicts per question, and
resolve each session `test-scenario`'s `verdict` with cited `evidence:` links — per
`90-meta/taxonomy.md`'s rule that any verdict other than `pending` needs at least one evidence
link.

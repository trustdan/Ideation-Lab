# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Full method in
[[design-sprint-2-agenda]]; classic five-day orientation in [[design-sprint-day-by-day]]. Not
a mandate — adapt to the room. -->

## Day 1 — Map & Sketch (~7h)
1. Long-term goal + sprint questions review (from the scenario hub) — ~30m
2. Expert interviews — ~90m
3. [[how-might-we]] — ~30m — reframe expert-interview insights into HMW stems (see
   prompt-pack.md)
4. Target pick (decider's call, on the map) — ~20m
5. [[crazy-8s]] — ~15m
6. Solo storyboards — ~45m

## Day 2 — Decide (~6h)
1. Silent critique (heat-map read of storyboards) — ~30m
2. [[dot-voting]] — ~15m
3. [[note-and-vote]] — ~15m — if the dot-vote doesn't cleanly resolve
4. Decider's supervote — ~10m
5. Storyboard the winner — ~90m

## Day 3 — Prototype (~6h)
1. Façade build — ~5h
2. Prep the [[five-act-interview]] script (coach prep-mode output — see interview-script.md)
   — ~30m

## Day 4 — Test (~5h)
1. Five [[five-act-interview]] interviews, ~1h each including reset — ~4.5h
2. Live synthesis of what was learned, against the sprint questions — ~30m

If any day runs over, consult [[time-recovery-playbook]] before improvising a cut.

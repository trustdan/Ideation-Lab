# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions for Day 1. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW make [core value prop] obvious in the first 10 seconds, with no onboarding?
- HMW let [target user] complete [key task] without ever hitting [known friction point]?
- HMW turn our riskiest untested assumption into something a five-day prototype can test?
- HMW borrow a pattern from a completely different industry to solve [core friction]?
- HMW make the "no" case (a user who churns or bounces) impossible instead of just less likely?

## Provocations (good for opening Monday or for target-pick)
- "If we could only test one assumption this week, which one — if wrong — kills the whole
  direction?"
- "What would a competitor with none of our technical debt build instead?"
- "What's the boring, obvious version of this that we've been avoiding because it's not
  exciting enough to pitch?"

## Warm-up question
- "Describe the last time you personally worked around this product instead of using it as
  intended — what did you actually do?"

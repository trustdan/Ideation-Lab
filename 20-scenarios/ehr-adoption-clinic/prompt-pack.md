# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW tell alert fatigue apart from click burden apart from a genuine liability concern, before
  proposing a fix for the wrong one?
- HMW make the documented path faster than the workaround, inside a 12-minute visit?
- HMW find the one clinician group where the tool already fits their workflow, and learn from
  them before mandating it elsewhere?
- HMW separate the small set of truly non-negotiable requirements from the larger set we've
  been treating as non-negotiable out of caution?
- HMW reduce clicks without reducing the clinical judgment the tool is supposed to support?
- HMW learn what the paper workaround or shadow chart captures that the tool doesn't?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If a malpractice attorney reviewed a chart from your busiest day, would the tool have helped
  you or slowed you down at the exact moment you needed speed?"
- "What alert have you dismissed so many times you no longer read it — and what would it take
  to make you read the next one?"
- "If this tool added thirty seconds to every visit, would that thirty seconds have been worth
  it for the last patient you saw?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one thing you still do on paper, or from memory, because the system doesn't make it
  fast enough to do it there."

# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW decide what the merged team needs to be able to do, before deciding whose process gets
  there?
- HMW keep the parts of each legacy process that were actually working, even if the other
  side's is "the standard" going forward?
- HMW give the team on the losing side of a decision a real voice in how the transition
  happens, even when they don't own the outcome?
- HMW surface disagreements early enough that they don't resurface three months into the
  integration as quiet non-compliance?
- HMW tell the difference between "different" and "worse" when comparing two processes that
  both worked for their original team?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If this merger is judged a failure eighteen months from now, what will people say was
  obvious in this room today that nobody said out loud?"
- "Which of our two processes would an outside consultant, with no loyalty to either legacy
  team, actually recommend — and why don't we believe them?"
- "What's one thing the other team does that you've privately admitted is better than how your
  team does it?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one process from your legacy team you'd genuinely be relieved to stop doing."

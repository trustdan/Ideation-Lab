# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW reduce our dependence on one funder without damaging that relationship?
- HMW find funding that fits our mission instead of bending our mission to fit funding?
- HMW turn our strongest program outcome into a case a new funder hasn't heard yet?
- HMW build a funder relationship for a program area we've never been funded in before?
- HMW tell the board what "diversify funding" actually requires us to say no to?
- HMW learn what the last funder who left told us, honestly, about why they left?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If our largest funder cut us next fiscal year with no warning, what would we already wish
  we'd started six months ago?"
- "What are we still doing only because 'that's what got funded last time' — not because it's
  still the right program?"
- "If a new organization with no track record in our space won the grant we're counting on,
  what would they have had to be right about?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one funding relationship we lost in the last two years where the real reason was never
  said out loud."

# Prompt pack
<!-- Use-case-specific HMW stems, provocations, and warm-up questions. -->

## How Might We stems
- HMW make the platform the path of least resistance instead of the workaround?
- HMW make the workaround harder to maintain than adopting the platform?
- HMW find the one business unit where adoption would compound fastest, and win them first?
- HMW turn "it's mandatory" into "it's obviously better," so mandate stops being the argument?
- HMW give business-unit delegates something they can sell to their own team, not just to us?
- HMW learn what the workaround does better than we do, before we ask anyone to give it up?

## Provocations (good for opening the room or for the premortem block)
- "If we deleted the mandate tomorrow, which business unit would keep using us anyway — and
  why don't the others?"
- "What did the last team that abandoned us for a workaround actually get that we don't give
  them?"
- "If a competitor's internal tool matched us feature-for-feature but shipped changes weekly
  instead of quarterly, would that be enough to lose the room?"

## Warm-up question
- "Name one workaround you know exists that nobody's told leadership about yet."

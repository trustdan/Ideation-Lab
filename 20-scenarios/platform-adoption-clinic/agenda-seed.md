# Agenda seed
<!-- Ordered bias for the facilitator — framework links + rough timeboxes. Not a mandate. -->
1. [[how-might-we]] — ~25m — reframe "why won't they adopt this" into concrete HMW stems (see
   prompt-pack.md) split evenly between platform-side and business-unit-side framings.
2. [[crazy-8s]] — ~20m — sketch concrete adoption interventions against the strongest 2–3 HMW
   stems. Push past "better docs" and "a training session" by round 2.
3. [[dot-voting]] — ~10m — converge the sketch pile to the top 2–3 candidate interventions.
4. [[bet-brief]] — ~35m — write up each shortlisted intervention as a one-page bet: the
   friction it removes, the target business unit, the resourcing ask.
5. [[premortem]] — ~25m — pressure-test the leading bet. Ask specifically what happens if the
   target business unit's lead changes mid-rollout.
6. [[commitment-round]] — ~30m — name the rollout owner, the first concrete action, and a
   90-day check-in date. Be explicit about what business-unit delegates can and can't commit
   to on their team's behalf.

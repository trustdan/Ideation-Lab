# Internal platform adoption clinic

**Who it's for:** A central platform/tooling team (design system, data platform, internal
dev-tools, shared services) inside a mid-to-large enterprise (500+ employees) whose product is
mandatory-in-theory but avoided-in-practice — business units route around it, maintain shadow
spreadsheets, or petition for exceptions every quarter.
**Outcome it produces:** One prioritized adoption bet — the specific friction it removes, the
business unit it targets first, and a rollout owner with a next-90-days plan. Not a roadmap; a
bet the platform team is willing to be measured against.
**Envelope:** half-day (~4 working hours) · 6–12 participants · in-person or remote, works
either way since the artifact is text-heavy.

## Notes for the facilitator
- The room usually mixes the platform team (who feel unappreciated) with business-unit
  delegates (who feel unheard). Name this split at the open — it de-personalizes what's
  coming.
- **Known failure mode #1:** the platform team treats "adoption" as a communications problem
  ("they just don't know the feature exists") rather than a design or incentive problem. Push
  past this in the HMW round — a stem like "HMW make the workaround harder than the platform"
  belongs on the wall, not just "HMW improve our docs."
- **Known failure mode #2:** a senior platform stakeholder pre-commits to a pet feature before
  divergence finishes, and the room quietly falls in line. Watch for early convergence and
  protect the sketch round explicitly.
- Business-unit delegates often have no formal authority to promise adoption on behalf of their
  team — be explicit in Commitment round about what they *can* actually commit to (a pilot, an
  intro to their team lead) versus what only the rollout owner can.
- Mandate can be a red herring: "it's mandatory" and "it's adopted" are different facts. If the
  room reaches for mandate-strengthening as the bet, treat that the same way
  [[defense-contractor-strategy]] treats "chase more IDIQs" — a safe answer that avoids the
  real one.

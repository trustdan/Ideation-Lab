# Agenda

## Arc statement

The room already has a muscle for risk-aversion (recompete pressure, single-prime exposure) —
the danger today is converging on a "safe" bet before any real alternatives exist. So the day
opens wide (reframe the problem, sketch broadly) before it narrows, and the room's rigor is
deliberately withheld until the [[premortem]] block, where it gets aimed at the 2 finalist
bets instead of killing divergence early. The afternoon trades hard cognitive work for a short
physical reset after lunch, then moves through a decide gate into a commitment round — landing
on 1–2 named bets, each with an owner and a dated first action, before the room tires.

## Materials & roles

**Facilitator:** runs the full day end-to-end (HMW → crazy-8s → dot-voting → bet-brief →
premortem → decide → commitment round). Holds the timer visibly and enforces it loudly during
crazy-8s per that framework's own note.

**Scribe:** HR/talent lead runs `/capture` continuously through the day (warm-up answers, HMW
stems, vote counts, premortem causes, final owner/action/date lines) — chosen because they're
least likely to be pulled into content arguments about capture strategy.

**Timekeeper:** VP Ops calls time at each block boundary and flags to the facilitator if a
block is running over budget.

**Prep before the day:**

- Print the 6 HMW stems and 3 provocations from `20-scenarios/defense-contractor-strategy/prompt-pack.md`
  as a handout / wall poster (don't read them cold — let people see them during HMW).
- Crazy-8s sketch sheets (8-panel fold) + markers, one set per person (12).
- Wall/whiteboard space for HMW clustering, sketch gallery, and the 2–3 bet briefs.
- Dot stickers, ~4 per person for the shortlist vote (candidates ÷ 3 per the framework note;
  expect ~6–10 sketch clusters → 3-4 dots each).
- A laptop projecting a live `50-actions.md` draft for the commitment round, so owner/action/
  date get written in front of the room, not reconstructed afterward.
- Printed premortem worksheet (one per breakout group) with the "it's 12 months later, this
  failed completely" prompt pre-filled per bet.

## Contingency

**If running 30 minutes behind:** cut the **gallery walk** (12:20, 20m). It's an energy-curve
nicety with an informal, non-required output — the room can walk-and-read the bet briefs
silently for 2 minutes while briefs go up on the wall, folded into the top of the premortem
block instead of standing alone.

**If running ahead:** add a **Finance feasibility gut-check** (~15–20m) after the commitment
round — Finance lead sanity-checks the budget/headcount ask behind each committed bet before
the room disperses, and any flag gets appended to the relevant `50-actions.md` line rather than
silently assumed. This directly extends the commit phase rather than reopening the decide gate.

## Run report

- **Scheduled:** 350 minutes of working blocks/breaks/lunch (09:00–14:50) against a stated
  window of 420 minutes (09:00–16:00) → **70 minutes buffer (16.7%)**, above the required 10%.
- **Frameworks used:** [[how-might-we]], [[crazy-8s]], [[dot-voting]], [[premortem]] — all
  from the existing library, group-size and timebox checked against headcount 12.
- **Stubs created:** [[bet-brief]] (converge, seed) and [[commitment-round]] (commit, seed) —
  the library had no framework for turning a vote tally into a comparable bet write-up, or for
  the owner/action/date closing ritual. Both flagged `status: seed`, `Steps: TODO`; added to
  `10-frameworks/index.md`. Whoever runs this scenario next should promote them to `ready` once
  the steps are field-tested.
- **Constraints bent:** the agenda-seed's per-block timeboxes (HMW ~30m, crazy-8s ~20m,
  dot-voting ~15m, premortem ~30m) were each stretched 5–15 minutes beyond both the seed's
  bias and the framework's baseline timebox, to account for the room being a full 12 people
  (top of every framework's group-size band) rather than a smaller team. Two extra blocks
  (bet-brief, commitment round) were inserted that the agenda-seed didn't call out — the seed
  stops at premortem, but the session's success criterion (named owner + dated first action)
  needed an explicit commit-phase block the seed doesn't cover.

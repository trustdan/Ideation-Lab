# The safer bet is doubling down on the existing prime relationship, not chasing an unproven adjacent agency

A steelman raised in the room against pursuing cyber range support at all: the existing prime relationship generates real revenue today, while an adjacent-agency cold start risks burning a quarter of BD capacity chasing a program that may never have been open to new entrants.

## Notes
- Raised specifically as the case for not doing the cyber-range-support bet, see [[pursue-cyber-range-support-in-adjacent-agency]].
- Landed hard in the room — several people reportedly recalculated their earlier enthusiasm for that bet after hearing the cold-start risk laid out this way.
- The room ultimately kept the bet alive rather than dropping it, but gated it on a kill test instead of proceeding on enthusiasm alone.

# Structural/ops bets need different evaluation criteria than BD capture bets

A bet built around an internal capability fix with a long payback horizon is a genuinely different kind of bet than a customer-facing capture play, and shouldn't be forced to compete against them under the same criteria.

## Notes
- Raised by the group writing up the cleared-talent-farm bet, which has no capture tactic at all and won't help this year's recompete pressure despite being a sound structural fix.
- The room agreed with the distinction and ultimately reclassified that bet as an ops initiative rather than a strategic bet, rather than judging it head-to-head against the two customer-facing bets. See [[cleared-talent-farm-sponsor-clearances-in-house]] for the bet this was raised about.

# Proposals lose when they're written to the customer's old CONOPS instead of their current one

A recent, smaller loss was traced to the proposal reading as if it understood the customer's previous concept of operations rather than their newly updated one.

## Notes
- Customer feedback on the loss was specific: the proposal read like the team didn't understand the new CONOPS, because it was effectively written to the old contract.
- Distinct from the staffing/relationship-building losses discussed alongside it — this is a bid-craft and customer-currency problem, not a capacity or past-performance problem. See [[loss-debriefs-recommendations-go-unactioned]] for the other two losses raised in the same warm-up round.

# Pursue cyber range support in an adjacent agency as our first adjacent-mission bet

Extend the company's existing cyber accreditation discipline into an adjacent agency's upcoming cyber range modernization program, entering as an accreditation subcontractor first.

## Notes
- Crazy-8s origin: the company already does cyber accreditation work; an adjacent agency has a cyber range modernization program coming, a natural crossover.
- Dot vote: 11 dots — the clear winner of the entire shortlist, advanced to bet-brief writing as Bet A.
- Bet brief: positioning is "we bring accredited cyber assessment discipline to range modernization, not just more bodies"; target is the adjacent agency's range modernization program office, which starts budget planning next fiscal year; capture tactic is to get a teaming call in front of the incumbent range integrator within 60 days, proposing as accreditation subcontractor first and prime later; resourcing ask was one BD lead part-time for 90 days plus travel budget for two site visits.
- Bet-brief's own flagged risk: zero existing relationships in this agency — a genuine cold start — with an explicit caution that the "clear winner" dot-vote result might reflect excitement more than buildability (see [[dot-vote-winners-may-reflect-excitement-not-buildability]]).
- Premortem causes of death, ranked: most likely is that the cold-start problem never gets solved — no warm intro ever materializes and the program office still doesn't know the company a year later; next is that a meeting happens but the program office's gate wants a prime with range-integration past performance specifically, and the accreditation angle isn't enough to get past it; least likely is that the program's own budget slips a year for reasons unrelated to the company.
- Kill test: a 2-week probe of the existing network for any warm path into the program office. If literally zero warm paths exist inside two weeks, treat that as a strong signal this is a cold-start fantasy rather than a strategy.
- A steelman was raised against pursuing this bet at all — see [[the-safer-bet-is-doubling-down-on-the-existing-prime]] — and it visibly shook the room's earlier enthusiasm.
- Decide gate: not dropped despite the risk, but explicitly gated — the kill test has to run and clear before any real BD capacity gets committed to the pursuit.

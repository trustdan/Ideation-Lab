# Sub to a different prime in the same mission area to spread single-prime risk

Take on subcontract work under a second, different prime active in the same mission area, purely to spread the risk of over-reliance on the current top prime.

## Notes
- Called "teaming reciprocity" in the room; sketched as a distinct move from deliberately courting a specific #2 player (see [[court-a-second-prime-relationship-deliberately]]) — this one is about spreading exposure across whichever prime has room, not building one deliberate relationship.
- The dot-vote tally doesn't list this as its own line item, so its vote count is unclear — it may have been folded into the neighboring "second prime relationship" tally or simply gone untracked as a separate option.

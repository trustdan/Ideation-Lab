# Loss debriefs keep naming the real fix, and the org keeps not doing it

Three separate contract losses each produced a clear, specific debrief recommendation that was never acted on before the same weakness resurfaced.

## Notes
- A depot sustainment recompete was lost two years ago; the debrief said the staffing plan looked thin on paper next to the incumbent's, and no bench was ever actually built — the same exposure is live again today.
- An adjacent-agency IDIQ bid was lost for having zero past performance in that agency; the debrief literally recommended "consider a teaming approach to establish past performance," and nothing came of it.
- A smaller, separate loss traced back to a proposal written to the customer's old contract and CONOPS rather than their newly updated one — kept as its own claim, see [[proposals-fail-when-written-to-outdated-conops]].
- Used as the day's framing device: the point of the session isn't just picking a new bet, it's actually acting on what's already been diagnosed at least twice before.

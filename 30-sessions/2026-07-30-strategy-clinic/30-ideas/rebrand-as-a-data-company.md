A blunter version of the data-layer idea: drop the services-labor identity entirely and rebrand around data.

## Notes
- Flagged in the room itself, at the moment it was sketched, as vague — more a positioning line than a real, buildable move — and explicitly worth challenging later.
- Landed in the bottom tier of the dot-vote tally (0-1 dots).
- Closely related to, but broader and less concrete than, [[sell-sustainment-data-analytics-not-labor]], which at least names a specific domain (sustainment) and a specific mechanism (sell the data layer).

# Deliberately court a second prime for 12 months before we need them

Pick the #2 player in our space and build a relationship with them well ahead of any actual need, positioning as the reliable sub who never no-bids.

## Notes
- Crazy-8s origin: pick the #2 player in our space and court them for 12 months before we need them.
- Dot vote: 9 dots, second-highest total, advanced to bet-brief writing as Bet B.
- Bet brief: positioning is "we are the reliable #2 sub who never no-bids you"; two candidate primes were identified by name, both currently competitors on 1-2 live bids — the writing group flagged that overlap as awkward on its face; capture tactic is an exploratory call with each candidate's capture leads, explicitly framed around the programs where the two firms aren't competing; resourcing ask is no new headcount, just senior BD time.
- Bet-brief risk flagged by the group: could look opportunistic or two-faced if it becomes known we're courting a prime while actively competing against them elsewhere.
- Premortem causes of death, ranked: most likely is that it never gets past a friendly lunch — no forcing function, both sides stay busy, and 12 months later it's still just goodwill with no actual teaming agreement; next is that the competing-bid awkwardness actually materializes and the relationship goes cold; least likely is that the candidate prime was never really looking for a sub in our domain in the first place.
- Kill test: in the very first exploratory call, ask for something small and concrete — "is there a specific upcoming task order you'd want a sub's help scoping" — rather than "let's stay in touch." If neither candidate prime gives a concrete answer on that first call, treat it as a signal not to let the relationship drift into one with no teeth.
- Decide gate: kept alive, with the concrete-ask kill-test fix built in from day one rather than added later as an afterthought.

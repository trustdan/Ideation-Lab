# A dot-vote winner can reflect excitement rather than buildability

A breakout group writing up the highest-voted bet flagged, unprompted, that a runaway dot-vote win might mean an idea is the most exciting one in the room rather than the most buildable one.

## Notes
- Raised specifically about the cyber-range-support bet (see [[pursue-cyber-range-support-in-adjacent-agency]]), which had zero existing relationships in the target agency despite being the clear dot-vote winner.
- Worth keeping as a standalone, generalizable caution for how this room — or any room running dot-voting — should weigh future landslide results against actual buildability.

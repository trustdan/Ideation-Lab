# Test scenario: Second-prime / small-prime diversification

## Given
The company currently derives the large majority of its revenue through one flagship prime
contract that is facing recompete pressure.

## When
Leadership commits capture capacity to direct small-prime awards under $10M and to
deliberately courting a second prime relationship, in parallel with defending the flagship
recompete.

## Then
Within 18 months, no single prime or contract accounts for the same concentration of revenue
as today.

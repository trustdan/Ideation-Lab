# Test scenario: Fractional cleared-talent bench

## Given
Cleared-talent attrition is a named risk to recompete win rate, and flexible remote work is
already identified as the top retention lever available.

## When
The company builds a fractional bench of cleared consultants (in-house sponsored clearances
and/or a retention structure tied to contract wins) rather than relying solely on flexible
work policy.

## Then
Cleared-talent retention improves enough that recompete proposal teams are not staffed at
risk.

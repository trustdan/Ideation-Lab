## Keep / Change / Try

**Keep**
- Running `/provoke` against the whole synthesis *before* anything hit `50-actions.md`. It
  caught a real, cheap, load-bearing gap — nobody had pulled the flagship recompete's own
  timeline — that a live, in-room premortem breakout did not surface. That's the process
  working exactly as designed.
- The bet-brief step (even as an ad-hoc stub) made the three finalists genuinely comparable
  before premortem — worth keeping in this scenario's agenda-seed going forward.

**Change**
- The gallery walk block produced no artifact. Either drop it from the scenario's
  `agenda-seed.md`, or explicitly assign a capture owner to it next time — an agenda block
  with no evidenced output is the exact smell the facilitator's own quality bar warns against.
- Two frameworks ([[bet-brief]], [[commitment-round]]) were invented ad hoc mid-agenda and are
  still `status: seed` with `Steps: TODO`. If this scenario runs again before those are
  field-tested and promoted, the next facilitator either reinvents them or runs on an
  unfinished method.
- The room's live commitment-round actions got substantively revised *after* the fact by
  `/provoke`. Useful this time, but it means the room dispersed believing a plan that didn't
  survive contact with pressure-testing — a gap between what people agreed to in the room and
  what's actually in `50-actions.md`.

**Try**
- Next time: run `/provoke` on each bet-brief right after it's written, *before* the decide
  gate — not only on the finished synthesis afterward — so the room's own decision benefits
  from the pressure-testing live, instead of `50-actions.md` quietly diverging from what the
  room thought it committed to.
- Assign a *named* owner for any "independent forcing function" action before the session
  closes, not a "(proposed)" placeholder — see Action follow-through, below.

# The product's value proposition should be legible within 10 seconds with zero onboarding

If a user cannot grasp why the product exists in the first 10 seconds, onboarding is already too late.

## Notes

- Originated as an HMW prompt surfaced from an expert interview with a support lead.
- HMW phrasing: "How might we make our value prop obvious in the first 10 seconds, with no onboarding?"
- Suggests the answer lives in the initial UI state or landing moment, not in an onboarding sequence.

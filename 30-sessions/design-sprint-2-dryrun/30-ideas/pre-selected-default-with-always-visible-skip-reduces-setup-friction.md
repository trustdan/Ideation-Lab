# A single pre-selected default choice paired with an always-visible skip is the right borrowable pattern for reducing setup friction

Linear's guided first-project wizard demonstrates that opinionated defaults plus a permanent escape hatch let users move forward without feeling trapped.

## Notes

- Source pattern: Linear's guided first-project wizard (lightning demo).
- Two specific mechanics to borrow: (1) a single option pre-selected by default — removes decision paralysis; (2) skip always visible — removes fear of commitment.
- Pattern is directly applicable to the integration-setup step identified in the sprint question.

## Keep / Change / Try

### Keep
- **Premortem → action pipeline.** The premortem output translated cleanly into a kill-test action with a specific user-recruitment task and a 3-day deadline. This tight coupling between red-teaming and commitment is worth preserving in the scenario template.
- **Silent dot voting before discussion.** The 6-2 split mentioned in the action context suggests the vote produced a genuine signal rather than groupthink; retaining silent-first convergence is warranted.
- **Timebox discipline.** 12 agenda blocks completed in a single day with two breaks and lunch — the schedule appears to have held.

### Change
- **Sketch-volume tracking.** The Crazy 8s target (~96 fragments for 12 people × 8 sketches) was not verified against any capture. Add a brief facilitator count at the end of the sketching block and log it as a single capture line.
- **Action breadth at commitment round.** A 12-person, multi-bet session produced 1 committed action. Either the session decided fewer bets than planned, or additional actions were agreed verbally but not recorded. The commitment-round block should end with a hard rule: every confirmed bet exits with ≥ 1 owner-dated action, even if it is only a "schedule the next meeting" placeholder.
- **Bet-brief count verification.** The agenda planned 2–3 briefs; only 1 is evidenced. Either confirm the others exist in synthesis or note them as open.

### Try
- **Structured action harvest.** At the close of the commitment round, circulate a shared doc pre-populated with one row per confirmed bet. Owners fill name + date before the room breaks. This prevents single-action under-recording.
  - *Suggested scenario edit:* Add a note to the `[[commitment-round]]` framework card: "Pre-populate one row per confirmed bet before the block begins — owners cannot leave a row blank."
- **Capture tagging by block.** With 8 captures across 12 agenda blocks, it is hard to trace which block generated which idea. Try tagging each capture with its source block (e.g., `#crazy8s`, `#premortem`) during the distill pass.
  - *Suggested framework note:* Add a `source-block:` field to the capture frontmatter template.

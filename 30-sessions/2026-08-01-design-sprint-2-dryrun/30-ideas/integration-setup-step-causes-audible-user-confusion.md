# The integration-setup step creates enough confusion that users verbalize uncertainty mid-flow

When a user stops and says aloud "wait, do I have to do this now?", the friction is not subtle — it is a blocking moment of doubt.

## Notes

- Interview 1 of 5, Act 3: participant paused at the integration-setup step and said out loud "wait, do I have to do this now?"
- This is a think-aloud signal of genuine confusion, not hesitation — the user questioned the mandatory nature of the step.
- Directly confirms sprint question ts-02.
- Supports the HMW framing around skipping or deferring integration setup.

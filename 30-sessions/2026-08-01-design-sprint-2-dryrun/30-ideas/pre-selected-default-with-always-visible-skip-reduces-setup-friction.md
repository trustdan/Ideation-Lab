# A pre-selected default choice paired with an always-visible skip removes the decision tax from setup flows

When the system makes a sensible choice on the user's behalf and escape is never hidden, users move forward faster and feel less trapped.

## Notes

- Borrowed pattern from Linear's guided first-project wizard (Lightning Demo source: Linear).
- Two specific mechanics worth replicating: (1) a single option pre-selected by default, and (2) a "skip" affordance that is always visible — never buried or conditional.
- This pattern is a candidate solution to the integration-setup friction identified in the HMW notes and interview findings.

# A subset of users will skip past guided onboarding entirely, suggesting guidance must survive that choice

Designing only for users who engage with the guided flow leaves self-directed users stranded in a blank workspace with no fallback support.

## Notes

- Interview 2 of 5, Act 3: user skipped straight to the blank workspace without reading the guided option.
- This contradicts sprint question ts-03 (which presumably assumed users would engage with the guided path).
- Implication: the blank-workspace experience itself may need ambient contextual cues — the two storyboard directions are not mutually exclusive.
- Creates productive tension with the dot-vote result favoring the guided-setup direction: the prototype should account for users who opt out of guidance.

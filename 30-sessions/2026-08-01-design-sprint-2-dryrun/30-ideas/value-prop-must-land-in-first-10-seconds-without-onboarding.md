# The product's value proposition should be self-evident within 10 seconds, requiring zero onboarding

If a user needs a guided tour to understand why the product matters, the UI itself is failing to communicate value.

## Notes

- Surfaced as a HMW prompt from the expert interview with the support lead: "HMW make our value prop obvious in the first 10 seconds, with no onboarding?"
- Implies that first-screen design must carry explanatory weight rather than deferring to tooltips or walkthroughs.

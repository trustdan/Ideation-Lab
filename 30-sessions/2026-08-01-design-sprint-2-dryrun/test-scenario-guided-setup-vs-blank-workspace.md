# Test scenario: guided setup chosen over blank workspace

## Given
A participant qualified by screener Q3 (has personally chosen to take or skip a guided
walkthrough during SaaS onboarding) is shown the prototype's choice screen — the screen that
presents both the guided setup path and the option to go straight to a blank workspace — with
both options clearly visible and neither pre-selected or visually weighted more than the other
(the design intent, not a prototype fidelity assumption; confirm with the Day 3 team before
the interview).

## When
In Act 4 (Task B) of the [[five-act-interview]], the interviewer says: *"You've just landed
here. What would you do next?"* — without naming or describing either option. The note-taker
logs which option the participant moves toward, how long it takes, and any verbalized
reasoning before they act.

## Then
The participant selects or moves toward the guided setup path (not the blank workspace), either
immediately or after brief deliberation. If they deliberate, any reasoning they verbalize is
logged as supporting evidence regardless of verdict — it is signal about the choice framing
that the Day 4 synthesis needs.

Pass signal: participant chooses guided setup without being prompted or redirected; ideally
articulates a reason that maps to the onboarding value prop (e.g. *"I want to know what I'm
doing before I start"*).
Fail signal: participant immediately skips to blank workspace, or chooses guided setup only
after visible confusion about what the screen is offering — hesitation of more than ~15
seconds with no verbalized reasoning is treated as a weak fail until the synthesis session
weighs it against the full five-interview pattern.
